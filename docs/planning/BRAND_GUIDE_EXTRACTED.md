## Design Observations

1. **Color Scheme**: Warm pink/rose (#C84869) as primary, complemented by cool blue (#213E8C)
2. **Typography**: Professional mix of Poppins (modern, geometric) for headings and Avenir (humanist) for body
3. **Contrast**: High contrast with black text on white backgrounds
4. **Button Style**: Ghost/outline style for secondary, filled for primary
5. **Brand Personality**: Friendly yet professional, energetic (pink) with trust (blue)

## Accessibility Notes

**Color Contrast Ratios** (estimated):

- Primary Rose (#C84869) on White: ~4.5:1 (AA compliant for large text)
- Black (#000000) on White: 21:1 (AAA compliant)
- Dark Gray (#333333) on White: ~12.6:1 (AAA compliant)
- Primary Rose on Dark Gray: May need testing
- White on Primary Rose: ~4.5:1 (AA compliant for large text)

**Recommendations**:

- Ensure primary rose (#C84869) is only used for large text or with white backgrounds
- Keep body text black or dark gray for maximum readability
- Test all color combinations with WCAG contrast checker

## Font Loading Strategy

Fonts to load:

1. **Poppins** (Google Fonts):
   - Weights: 200 (ExtraLight), 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold)
2. **Avenir LT W01 35 Light** (Commercial font):
   - Requires license or web font service
   - Alternative: Use Avenir Next from system fonts or substitute with Montserrat
3. **DIN Next W01 Light** (Commercial font):
   - Requires license
   - Alternative: Substitute with similar geometric sans (e.g., "Inter", "DM Sans")

**Fallback Stack**:

```css
--heading-font:
  "Poppins", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif;
--body-font:
  "Avenir Next", "Avenir", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif;
--small-font:
  "Inter", "DIN Next", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif;
```
